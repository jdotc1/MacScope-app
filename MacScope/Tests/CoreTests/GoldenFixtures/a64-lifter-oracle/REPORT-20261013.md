How to read this:
- PASS: every expected output and every unchanged-state invariant matched.
- FAIL: an output or invariant differed, or the IR's C is undefined for the input.
- UNLIFTED: the lifter emitted `.unknown` for the instruction.
- UNSUPPORTED: valid IR the evaluator does not model: NZCV/FPSR-only outputs (MacIR has no flag state), vector lanes, non-default FPCR, unknown intrinsics.
- MacIR is evaluated as the C it renders: `xN` is `int64_t`, `wN` is `int32_t`, immediates are `int64_t`, and `>>`, `/` and comparisons follow the operand types. Loads and stores move their register's width, because MacIR records no access width.
- NZCV/FPSR outputs on a case that also writes registers are listed as unmodeled and are not counted as mismatches.
- FAIL tags: `signedness` means the case passes with every signed/unsigned decision flipped (faithful but ambiguous IR). `access-width(Nu|Ns)` means it passes with an N-byte zero/sign-extending access. `c-undefined` means the C is undefined where A64 is defined.
- Flag consumers (`csel`, `cset`, …) that lift to `.unknown` alone are re-run behind a synthesized `cmp` that produces the fixture's exact NZCV.

## MacIR fidelity: width and signedness (§4.78)

MacIR records no signedness on `>>`, `/` and relational conditions (`lsr`/`asr` → `>>`, `sdiv`/`udiv` → `/`, `hs`/`ge` → `>=`), no access width on loads and stores (`ldrb`/`ldrh`/`ldr` → one `load`), and no NZCV/FPSR state. These FAILs pass once that one decision is flipped, so they are fidelity gaps in the IR, not wrong arithmetic.
